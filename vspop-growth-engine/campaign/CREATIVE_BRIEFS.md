# Three video briefs — "Another Day" (VS Pop™)

> **Replaced (12 November 2026).** The promoted track is now "Beneath the Last Autumn Moon". The first video will be cut from the official video; new briefs for the other two will be written when paid ads resume. See `../PLAYBOOK.md`.

**Status: briefs for approval. No video has been made.** The song has not been listened to in preparing these, so the timings are placeholders: where a brief says "the hook", you choose the bars.

## Rules for all three

- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 15–25 seconds, MP4.
- **Music:** the released master of "Another Day", audible from the first frame, no spoken introduction. Use the audio in the video file itself, not Instagram's music sticker.
- **Rights:** only music, artwork and footage owned by VS POP RECORDS LLP or CR Srikanth. No stock clips of recognisable people, no other artists' names or music, no borrowed film footage.
- **No personal photograph is required.** The website avoids one by choice, and each brief below works without a face. If you later want to appear, brief 3 is the natural place.
- **Safe area:** keep words and anything important out of the top 270 px and the bottom 670 px, where Instagram puts its own buttons and captions.
- **Words on screen:** at most two short lines at a time, large, high contrast. Many people watch with the sound off for the first second.
- **Ending:** the last two seconds show the artwork with "Another Day · CR Srikanth" and "Listen on Spotify". The words must match the landing page.
- **No claims:** no chart positions, stream counts or "as heard on" lines in the ads.
- **Three different song sections.** Southworth reports one case where the chorus cost three times as much per conversion as the verse, so each brief takes a different part of the song. That way the test also shows which section pulls best.

## Brief 1 — Music hook (`a1-hook`)

**Idea:** the strongest eight bars, and nothing in the way of them.

- **Song section:** the main hook or chorus, starting exactly on its downbeat.
- **Picture:** the "Another Day" artwork, slowly pushed in, with a simple moving waveform or bars that follow the music. One line of the lyric appears in time with the vocal, if the hook has words.
- **Opening second:** the artwork already full-screen and the hook already playing.
- **On-screen words:** the sung line; then "VS Pop™ = Very Smooth Pop".
- **Primary text:** `Very Smooth Pop. "Another Day" is out now.`
- **What it tests:** whether the song sells itself with no story around it.

## Brief 2 — Emotional and cinematic (`a2-cinematic`)

**Idea:** the feeling of the artwork, brought to life: dusk over water, a city on the horizon, a single rose.

- **Song section:** the most atmospheric passage, such as the introduction or a verse that builds, ending as the chorus arrives.
- **Picture:** footage you own or shoot on a phone: a sunset, light on water, a skyline, a hand setting down a rose. Slow, steady shots of three to four seconds each, graded warm orange to match the cover. If no footage exists, animate the artwork in layers (sky, skyline, water, rose) with gentle parallax.
- **Opening second:** the most striking image first, not a fade from black.
- **On-screen words:** one line only, mid-video, for example "For the end of a long day." You know the song's meaning; replace this with a line that is true to it.
- **Primary text:** `For the drive home, the last light, the long exhale. "Another Day" by CR Srikanth.`
- **What it tests:** whether mood and picture bring in listeners who would scroll past a plain cover.

## Brief 3 — How it is made (`a3-production`)

**Idea:** a composer's session. Classical training and pop production, shown on the screen where the song was built.

- **Song section:** a part where layers enter one by one, or the bridge.
- **Picture:** a screen recording of the real "Another Day" session: the arrangement scrolling as it plays, then tracks soloed in turn (strings, then keys, then drums, then everything). Hands on a keyboard can be cut in, with no face needed. Use the real project, not a mock-up.
- **Opening second:** the full arrangement on screen, already playing, with the line "This is what a VS Pop™ song looks like."
- **On-screen words:** labels as each layer enters ("strings", "keys", "the drop"), then "Composed and produced by CR Srikanth".
- **Primary text:** `Written like a score, produced like a pop record. "Another Day", VS Pop™.`
- **What it tests:** whether the composer-producer identity attracts people who then follow the artist rather than hearing one song.

## What to send back for approval

For each brief: the finished MP4, the exact start and end time in the song, and confirmation that every picture in it is yours. The three files should be named `a1-hook.mp4`, `a2-cinematic.mp4` and `a3-production.mp4`, matching the ad names.
