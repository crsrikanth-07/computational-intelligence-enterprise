A world of music, words and ideas

One creative mind. Many forms of expression.

CR Srikanth, also known as Ravi Chandra Srikanth Cherukupalli, is a composer, recording artist, fiction writer and creative-technology innovator.

His work moves between classical composition and contemporary pop, between literary storytelling and the building of new tools for musical creation. From orchestral scores to published fiction, and from VS Pop™ to the CuTTI tools, it explores the different ways imagination can take form.

The Attic Record

Fiction · 10 October 2026

Lindenfeld, August 1958. Sent to the attic for a tablecloth, a seven-year-old finds an old gramophone and listens alone to Haydn’s “Emperor” Quartet. Decades later, after his mother’s funeral, he learns what that afternoon meant to her.

Tales from Bellavere and Lindenfeld

Short-story collection · 2026

Linked stories from two small European towns that appear on no map. Across generations, families pass down loyalties, recipes, music, houses, debts and secrets: a mechanic, a chocolatier, a struggling theatre company, a singing palazzo, and a boy changed by a forgotten gramophone record.
