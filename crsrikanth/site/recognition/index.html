April 2025

Radio

FM Airplay in Chicago, and 19 College Radio Adds

“Dancing in the Dark” was tracked by Mediabase on WCPY-FM Dance Factory, Chicago, with 57 spins between 18 April and 3 May 2025. In the same month the Official Electronic EP was added by 19 NACC-reporting college stations in the United States and Canada, reaching medium rotation at WDWN, WSUW and KXSU.
