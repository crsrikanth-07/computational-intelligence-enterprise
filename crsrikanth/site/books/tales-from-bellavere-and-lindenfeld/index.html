Short-story collection

Tales from Bellavere and Lindenfeld

Linked stories from two small European towns that appear on no map. Across generations, families pass down loyalties, recipes, music, houses, debts and secrets: a mechanic, a chocolatier, a struggling theatre company, a singing palazzo, and a boy changed by a forgotten gramophone record.
