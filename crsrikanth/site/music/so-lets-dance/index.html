About the song

So Lets Dance

VS Pop™, short for Very Smooth Pop, is the name CR Srikanth gives to his contemporary songs: smooth melodies and pop songwriting, electronic production, cinematic textures and orchestral colour, drawing on classical, Carnatic and Hindustani influences.
