Contemporary

VS Pop™: Very Smooth Pop

VS Pop™, short for Very Smooth Pop, is the name CR Srikanth gives to his contemporary songs: smooth melodies and pop songwriting, electronic production, cinematic textures and orchestral colour, drawing on classical, Carnatic and Hindustani influences.

Listen to VS Pop™

Classical and Orchestral

Compositions credited to Ravi Chandra Srikanth Cherukupalli: symphonies such as Pi and Hope, string quartets, nocturnes, sonatas, fugues, études, waltzes and symphonic serenades, catalogued by opus number.
