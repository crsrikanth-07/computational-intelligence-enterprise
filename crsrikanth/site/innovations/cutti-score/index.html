CuTTI Score

From Paper to an Editable, Playable Score.

A desktop app that turns printed music into scores to edit, play and use: from PDFs, scans and phone photos, and from recordings too.

What it doesScan, photo, audio

Three ways in

Scan Score. Photo Score. Audio Score.

Scan Score reads PDFs and scanned pages. Photo Score straightens a phone photo of a page and corrects its lighting. Audio Score turns a recording or a live performance into notation. In every case the result is a score that can be reviewed, edited, played and exported.

What it does

Read, Review, Edit, Play, Export.

Reads printed music

From PDFs, scans and phone photos: it straightens the page, corrects the lighting, and recognises clefs, keys, meters, notes, rests, chords, ties, slurs, dynamics and repeats.

Shows what it is unsure about

Uncertain spots are marked, so only those need reviewing and they can be fixed quickly.

Editing with undo

Pitch, rhythm, accidentals, ties, transposition, key, meter and tempo, with note entry from the computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard.

Playback

A built-in synth or SoundFonts, with a following cursor, loop and metronome.

Audio to score

Turns a recording or a live performance into notation.

Exports

MusicXML, MIDI, PDF, PNG and WAV. A score corrected here becomes a song in CuTTI Studio with one line.

Status

Private, and in Active Development.

CuTTI Score is built for the composer’s own work and is not on sale. It works hand in hand with CuTTI Studio: a score corrected here becomes a song in the DAW with one line.

Status: both tools are private, built for the composer’s own work, and in active development.
