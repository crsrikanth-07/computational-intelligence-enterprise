## Spelling and editions (decided; change in the content files if needed)

- **"Lets Dance":** the store titles are "So Lets Dance(VS Pop™)" (the release) and "Lets Dance(VS Pop™)" (its only track), with no apostrophe. They are one recording. The site uses the store spelling. Fix it at the distributor first if an apostrophe is wanted.
- **Release titles:** the site shows each title without the "(VS Pop™)" suffix and puts "VS Pop™ single" on the line below. The full store title stays in the image description.
- **Lindenfeld:** the October 2026 collection is *Lindenfeld*. The earlier paperback is listed as printed, *Lindelfeld*.
- **Story count:** the ebook blurb says twelve stories and the paperback blurb says ten. The site says "linked stories", with no number.
- **Euro Indie #6:** this is a weekly chart (week 6, published 17 February 2026), not a monthly February chart.
- **Moon Phase Radio #1:** two weekly charts (13 and 20 January 2025), described by the station as based on listener interactions.
- **IMSLP:** "Beneath the Last Autumn Moon" is a recording, not an IMSLP score. Possible duplicates on IMSLP: "Orchestral Suite" vs "Abandoned Station, Op. 10 No. 26", and "May be Sonata, Op. 9 No. 4" vs "Violin Sonata No. 4 'May Be', Op. 9 No. 4".
- **Not found under your name on Apple Music, Spotify or Deezer:** "Ghosa (The Call)", "Abandoned Station", "PRYZM", "Shanthi", "Bees Orchestra" and "Of Shadows Gathering…". The site mentions Ghosa and Abandoned Station only through their chart records and videos.
