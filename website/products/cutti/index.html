02

Why CuTTI

Start with the Music, Not the Setup.

Before the creative work begins, there is the session: creating tracks, choosing sounds, naming, routing, laying out sections, setting levels. It can take hours, and it is where many producers, especially those starting out, get stuck.

CuTTI does that groundwork for you. You start with a complete, organised session and spend your time on the music.

03

How it works

Five Steps from a Sentence to a Session.

  1. 01 Describe it.

    “A dark pop track with 32 instruments, a clean structure, breaks and a big chorus.” Plain words are enough.

  2. 02 CuTTI builds it.

    Your AI assistant writes the song plan, and CuTTI builds it in Logic Pro: tracks, built-in instruments, regions cut at every section, markers, tempo and key.

  3. 03 Every step is checked.

    CuTTI reads Logic back after each step and reports what it verified. Nothing counts as done just because a command was sent.

  4. 04 Mixed and mastered.

    CuTTI sets a static mix and masters the song with Logic’s own tools, ready for streaming.

  5. 05 Yours to finish.

    Every track, region and note is editable. Ask for changes in plain words, such as “make the chorus bigger”, or undo them.

04

What you get

A Session You Would Have Built Yourself, Ready to Finish.

  • A real Logic Pro session

    Saved as a new project. Not audio files, not stems.

  • Built-in Logic Pro instruments

    Nothing extra to buy beyond Logic’s own sound library.

  • A producer’s layout

    Purpose-built, named tracks, with regions only where a part plays, cut at every section.

  • A streaming-ready master

    A static mix and a master at −14 LUFS with true peak at or below −1 dBTP, made with Logic’s Mastering Assistant and Adaptive Limiter.

  • Many styles

    38 genre profiles, from pop, EDM and hip-hop to cinematic, plus Indian styles: Bollywood romantic, dance and Sufi, with raga-aware melodies and Indian instruments from Logic’s library.

  • Follow-up edits and undo

    Change a section, an instrument or the energy, and undo any change.

  • Original music every time

    CuTTI writes new parts for each song. A reference is treated as a style brief; it never copies an existing song.

Questions

Does CuTTI make the music for me?

It builds a complete, original first version: arrangement, sounds, mix and master. You shape it from there; the creative decisions stay yours.

Do I need an API key?

No. CuTTI works with your own Claude Desktop account.

Will it change my existing projects?

No. CuTTI works only on projects it creates and never overwrites a file.

Can I edit everything?

Yes. It is a normal Logic Pro session: every track, region and note can be changed.

Is the music original?

Yes. CuTTI writes new parts for every song and never copies existing music.

How long does a song take?

In our demo, a 32-instrument song took about 13 minutes to build and about 15 more to mix and master. Smaller songs are quicker.

Does it work on Windows?

No. Logic Pro runs only on Mac, so CuTTI does too.
