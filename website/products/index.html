Product

Value Lens Private beta

Decision support and financial controls over ERP data.

Value Lens reads commercial and cost facts through read-only tools, computes results in deterministic code under versioned policies, and raises evidence-backed cases that a reviewer accepts or dismisses with a reason. Its first use case is margin leakage in order-to-cash: where an enterprise lost money it should not have lost, and why.

  • Read-only by designValue Lens never writes to source systems.

  • Deterministic numbersCalculations in exact integer arithmetic under a versioned policy.

  • Evidence per caseSources, calculation and decision kept together.

  • People decideAgents explain; a reviewer accepts or dismisses.
