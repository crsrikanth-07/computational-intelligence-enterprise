How it works

How It Works.

  1. 01

    Upload.

    Upload your iFlow packages and documentation. Train Index and Train Docs build the retrieval index; nothing is sent outside the workbench.

  2. 02

    Ask.

    Type the request in plain English. iFlow Generation Mode targets Integration Suite; the same bar answers code and functional questions.

  3. 03

    Generate.

    The Suite drafts the artefact from your library and the trained knowledge, with the operation type, objects and mappings made explicit.

  4. 04

    Review.

    A certified architect reviews every artefact before it enters a landscape. The review record travels with the artefact into the evidence pack.

07

Questions

Does the Suite deploy iFlows or transport code by itself?

No. It generates artefacts. Deployment and transports stay with your architects and your change process.

Which models does it use?

Local open-weight models on your infrastructure by default, or a hosted model you choose. The model never sees data you have not uploaded to the workbench.

Can it learn our conventions?

Yes. Train Index and Train Docs build the retrieval index from your own iFlow packages and documentation, so generated artefacts follow your patterns.

Is it available today?

It is in use in SVLS LABS delivery and available to customers on request. Book a demo and we will scope a deployment in your landscape.
