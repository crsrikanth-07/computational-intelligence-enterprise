Questions

Does the Suite deploy flows or transport code by itself?

No. It generates artefacts. Deployment and transports stay with your architects and your change process.

Which models does it use?

Local open-weight models on your infrastructure by default, or a hosted model you choose. The model never sees data you have not uploaded to the workbench.

Can it learn our conventions?

Yes. It is trained on your own artefacts and documentation, so generated work follows your patterns.

Is it available today?

It is in use in SVLS LABS delivery and available to customers on request. Book a demo and we will scope a deployment in your landscape.

What does a generated integration flow contain?

A complete Integration Suite package. It holds the flow definition, the Groovy scripts it needs and the manifest. They are assembled in the form the CPI import dialog expects. An architect reviews it before it enters a landscape.

Does a language model write the integration flow?

No. The artefact is assembled by rule from a matched template in your library or from a built-in skeleton, and the Groovy comes from a pattern library. The language model, local or hosted, explains the result. The Suite keeps working when the model is offline and says so on screen.
