The Story Since 2020

We started in 2020 as an enterprise integration team with a narrow ambition: to be the engineers companies keep after go-live, because the work holds. Our key architects brought 25+ years of ERP delivery experience. The first years were integration work: flows built from a pattern library, a written contract per interface and a read-back check after every run.

From there the work moved into the cloud around the ERP core and into governed agentic AI, where we built the control model that now runs through everything we make: agents act through defined tools, every step is verified, and a person decides. Value Lens, our margin leak finder for order-to-cash, came out of that work.

The same discipline carried into a second portfolio. Our team also composes, produces and writes, and CuTTI is what happens when that practice meets our engineering: a production assistant that builds a real, editable Logic Pro session from one request and checks every step it takes.

Today SVLS LABS is one company with two portfolios and one standard: software that does the groundwork, shows its work, and leaves the decisions to people.

Research and Publications

Computational Intelligence for Enterprise Systems: Advanced Optimization Algorithms with Working Prototypes.

Technical Writing, Open Engineering and Fiction.

Our team authored the technical book Computational Intelligence for Enterprise Systems: Advanced Optimization Algorithms with Working Prototypes. It covers supply-chain optimisation with working prototypes: genetic optimisation for supply networks, simulated annealing for transport modes, particle swarm methods for inventory policy and a primer on Markov chains and Monte Carlo methods, closing with a staged deployment blueprint that keeps a human override in place.

Our open engineering work includes reference designs for agent-to-agent interoperability, retrieval over enterprise documentation and process models, deterministic integration flow generation, and probabilistic music models built on Markov chains and constraint-based decoding. Our team has also published five works of fiction.
