About

An Architecture and Engineering Company for Complex Enterprise Landscapes.

SVLS LABS LLP was founded in 2020 by enterprise architects with long experience in ERP, integration and enterprise software. We work across vendors and platforms, and we build software where the platforms stop.

Explore our services Contact us
02

Why we exist

Landscapes Grow One Decision at a Time.

An ERP platform, then a second one after an acquisition. Integrations added under deadline. Custom code that outlived its authors. Cloud services, one project at a time. Now AI agents. Each decision made sense when it was taken. Together they are hard to change.

We started SVLS LABS to bring architecture discipline to that work: understand the landscape as it is, agree what it should become, and change it in steps that can be verified. Our key architects bring more than 25 years of ERP delivery experience, alongside integration, cloud, software architecture and AI governance.

Architecture engagements show us the problems that recur. Engineering solves them. Where a solution repeats, we turn it into a product. Value Lens began that way.
