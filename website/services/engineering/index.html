03

When this matters

When Configuration Is Not Enough.

  • A critical process lives in spreadsheets and email

  • The same integration is rebuilt project after project

  • Decisions depend on data from several systems

  • Custom code in the core is the only way anyone has found

  • Teams need enterprise data without access to the core

  • An internal tool needs to become a supported product

04

Questions

Do you build on our platforms or yours?

On yours by default: your cloud, your identity provider, your repositories. Deliverables and code are yours.

How do you keep quality measurable?

Contracts before code, tests against exact answers, read-back verification after integration runs, and documentation and runbooks your team can operate.

Do you use AI-assisted engineering?

For repeatable work such as scaffolds, mapping specifications and tests. Every artefact is reviewed by an engineer before it reaches your landscape.
