What we deliver

Architecture That Survives Contact with Delivery.

Every recommendation comes with its reasoning, its dependencies and the decision it needs, written down so it can be reviewed and challenged.

01

Future-State Architecture

The target landscape across applications, integration, data and security, with the transition states between today and the target.

Deliverablescurrent-state map, target architecture, transition states, decision records.

02

Technology Roadmaps

Initiatives sequenced by dependency, risk and value, aligned to business priorities rather than vendor release cycles.

Deliverablesinitiative backlog, sequencing, investment view, risk per stage.

03

Application Portfolio Rationalisation

What runs, what overlaps, what each application costs to keep. Retain, replace, consolidate or retire, with the reasoning for each.

Deliverablesapplication inventory, overlap analysis, disposition per application.

04

Platform Architecture

Reference architectures for ERP, integration, data and cloud platforms, and clear ownership of every boundary between them.

Deliverablesreference architectures, boundary and ownership map, non-functional requirements.

05

Architecture Governance and Operating Models

Principles, standards, review forums and decision rights that keep the landscape coherent after the programme ends.

Deliverablesarchitecture principles, standards catalogue, review process, target operating model.

06

Technical Due Diligence

An independent view before an acquisition, carve-out, investment or major programme: architecture, technical debt, risk and the cost to change.

Deliverablesfindings report, risk register, remediation estimate.

03

When this matters

Signs the Landscape Needs an Architecture, Not Another Project.

  • Two or more ERP platforms after an acquisition

  • A platform renewal or end-of-support date is approaching

  • Point-to-point integrations nobody can fully map

  • AI initiatives are starting without architecture ownership

  • A programme needs an independent design authority

  • The board or an investor needs an independent technical view

04

Questions

Are you tied to a platform vendor?

No. We recommend what fits your landscape, including keeping what already works. Our advice is not tied to licence sales.

Do you replace our architecture team?

No. We work with your architects, add capacity and an outside view where it helps, and leave the artefacts and decisions with your team.

How long does a future-state architecture take?

A focused landscape can be covered by the two-week Architecture Assessment. A full enterprise architecture is scoped with you, at a fixed price per stage.
