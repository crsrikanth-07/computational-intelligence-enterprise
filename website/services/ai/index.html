03

When this matters

When AI Starts Touching Systems of Record.

  • Agents are being given access to ERP, CRM or finance systems

  • Several AI vendors are being piloted at once

  • Internal audit or security has asked how AI actions are controlled

  • A pilot worked and now needs to run in production

  • Numbers in AI output need to be trusted

  • Nobody owns the architecture of AI in the enterprise

05

Questions

Do you build models?

We apply established methods, from retrieval-augmented generation to optimisation and probabilistic models, where they are the right tool. Business calculations stay in deterministic code.

Which AI platforms do you work with?

We are vendor-neutral. The control model sits between any agent and your systems, so it holds whichever models or agent frameworks you choose.

Where can we see the control model working?

In Value Lens, our decision-support product, now in private beta on synthetic ERP-like data. We walk through its artefacts and review log in a discovery call.
