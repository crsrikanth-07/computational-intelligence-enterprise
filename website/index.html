06

Governed AI on SAP BTP

AI in Your ERP Is a Control Question Before It Is a Productivity Question.

The same agent that can raise a purchase order can raise the wrong one. Our agents explain; your people decide. The numbers never come from the model.

Read-before-write

The agent fetches the current SAP state before it proposes anything, and its proposal is diffed against that state.

PreventsActing on a stale document. Writing over a change a person made five minutes ago.

Confirm-to-act

Every create or update is allowlisted by tool and field, shown to a person for review, and executed only with an expiring confirmation token.

PreventsUnsupervised writes. Replay of an old approval. An agent reaching a field it was never given.

Verify-after

After every action, SAP is read back and the result is compared to the intent. The comparison goes into the audit trail with the case.

PreventsSilent partial failures. "The agent said it did it." An audit you cannot reconstruct.
