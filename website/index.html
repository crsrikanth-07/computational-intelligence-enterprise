05

AI and governance

Enterprise AI Should Not Bypass Architecture.

Agents that read and change enterprise systems are integrations with judgement. They need the same contracts, controls and evidence as any other integration.

01

Governed Interfaces

Agents reach systems through defined tools and APIs with scopes and limits, never through shared credentials or direct database access.

02

Deterministic Where It Matters

Prices, margins, quantities and tax come from versioned, tested code. The model explains a result; it does not produce it.

03

Validated, Approved, Verified

Every action is validated before it runs, approved by a named role when the risk requires it, and read back independently afterwards.
