## 4. Page structure

- **Home:** hero with the landscape topology; the problem statement; six capabilities; the engagement model; "Enterprise AI should not bypass architecture"; products from recurring problems; facts (2020, 25+ years, one contract per interface, two-week assessment); call to action.
- **Services overview:** one row per practice with its scope list, plus the assessment as the entry row; the shared method.
- **Practice pages:** hero; six deliverable cards; "when this matters"; a checklist where one exists (ERP, AI); questions; related practices; call to action.
- **Architecture Assessment:** what we review (10 items) and what you receive (6 deliverables); how it runs (Scope, Map, Analyse, Read out); who should be involved; questions.
- **AI Governance:** seven principles (governed interfaces; deterministic calculations; read before write; validate before execution; approve by risk; verify independently; auditable evidence and state); the governance stack; what changes for the CIO, security and finance; questions.
- **Products:** how products emerge (engagement → engineering → product); Value Lens; six problem areas, stated as problem areas and not as products.
- **Approach:** five stages with their artefacts; standards; where AI assists and where it never decides; security and confidentiality.
- **About:** why we exist (landscapes grow one decision at a time); four commitments; what we work across; where we are. The page is not about the founder.

## 5. Calls to action

- Primary: **Start an Architecture Assessment** (header, home, every practice page)
- Secondary: **Book a discovery call** ("A conversation with an architect, not a salesperson. Replies within one business day. NDA before discovery.")
- Product: **Request Value Lens beta access**
- AI: **Review your AI architecture**
- Download: company overview PDF; Governed Agent Control Checklist PDF

## 6. Visual direction

Charcoal and white, with one restrained coral accent used for the governed path, focus and key lines. Architectural grids and hairline rules, a system-topology diagram (agents and channels → a governed integration layer → systems of record), ruled checklists and mono labels. No music, creative or robot imagery, no stock photography, no colourful gradients.

## 7. Taken from the brief, adapted, or deferred

- **Taken:** vendor-neutral positioning, the six-practice structure, the assessment as the primary entry, the AI governance page, products positioned as emerging from recurring problems, the five-stage method, restrained language.
- **Adapted:** "Integration" and "Cloud" are one practice, so the cloud work keeps a home without a thin page. The engagement model is the same five stages everywhere (Discover, Architect, Engineer, Govern, Evolve) rather than two different sets.
- **Deferred:**
  - *Insights:* an empty Insights section would weaken credibility. Add it once three or four real articles are written; the topics are ready (architecture notes, ERP modernisation, integration, AI governance).
  - *Industries:* the brief itself warns against claiming industry depth without evidence. Add industries once named references or case studies can be shown.
- **Kept facts only:** founded 2020; 25+ years of ERP delivery experience across key architects; a two-week, fixed-scope, fixed-price assessment; Value Lens in private beta. No invented clients, figures or certifications.
